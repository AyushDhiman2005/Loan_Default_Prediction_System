Financial institutions process thousands of loan applications every day. 

Approving loans without accurately assessing the applicant's credit risk can lead to significant financial losses when borrowers fail to repay their loans. 

Conversely, rejecting too many applicants may result in lost business opportunities and reduced customer satisfaction.

Traditional credit assessment methods often rely on manual evaluation and predefined rules, which may not effectively capture complex patterns in customer data. 

As the number of applications increases, banks require a faster, more consistent, and data-driven approach to evaluate creditworthiness.

This project aims to develop a machine learning model that predicts the likelihood of a borrower defaulting on a loan based on historical customer and loan information. By identifying high-risk applicants before loan approval, the bank can make more informed lending decisions, reduce default rates, minimize financial losses, and improve overall risk management.
